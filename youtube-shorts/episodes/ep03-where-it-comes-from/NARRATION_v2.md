# Episode 3 narration, v2: a story, not a caption track

Same voice (Andrew). Facts checked: TEM-1 was first reported in 1965 from an *E. coli* isolated in Athens in
1963 from a patient named Temoneira (hence TEM), on a transferable plasmid; by 1974 the same gene was in
*Haemophilus influenzae* and by 1976 in *Neisseria gonorrhoeae*. The F pilus is literally called the sex pilus;
conjugation is bacterial sex. Nothing below is invented.
