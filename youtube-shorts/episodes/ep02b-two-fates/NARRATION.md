Landscape, 1920x1080, 99 s. Calm, unhurried delivery, one idea per sentence.
Timecodes are the real ones in `renders/two_fates_v2.mp4` (`scripts/assemble_two_fates.py`,
`times` list). Pauses between lines are intentional; the pictures carry those beats.
About 200 words, roughly 95 s of speech.

Current voice: Azure neural `en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural` (warm, confident), rate -6 %,
pitch -2 Hz, generated by `scripts/make_narration.py` with the free `edge-tts` package into
`narration/01.wav` .. `15.wav`. To change the voice: `python scripts/make_narration.py en-GB-RyanNeural --force`.

## Recording notes

- Record at 48 kHz, mono is fine; leave 2 s of room tone at the start for noise profiling.
- Read each line as its own take; the assembler places lines by timecode, so timing does not need to be perfect.
- Export one file per line as `narration/NN.wav` (01 to 15), or one continuous file plus this table; either works.
- The music is ducked under speech automatically; no need to leave extra gaps for it.
